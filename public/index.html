Will I be charged when beta ends?

No. Paid subscriptions will require an explicit purchase. Pricing and final plan limits will be announced before paid plans open.

What if I’m offline?

Beta device management works offline once your development tools and runtimes are installed. Tool downloads and checking for updates need internet access.

How will subscriptions work?

You’ll choose a plan and confirm your purchase before anything is charged. Paid plans will include purchase recovery and a place to manage or cancel your subscription. Payments are not available in this beta.

Does React Native work?

You can prepare Android emulators and iOS simulators for use in your own tools. React Native launch, Metro and debug integration are not part of this beta.
